Getting Started Guide

Welcome to Folio — Your Research Synthesis Workspace

Follow these 4 steps to read, annotate, and synthesize your downloaded research papers:

1. Open & Color-Code PDFs

Click + Open PDF (or drag & drop files) to open multiple papers as browser tabs and assign each a distinct color tag.

2. Highlight Claims & Critiques

Select any text inside a PDF tab to tag it as a Claim, Critique, Method, or Quote with margin notes.

3. Build Your Synthesis Matrix

Switch to Reference Board & Matrix to compare papers side-by-side, draft paraphrases, and click citations to jump straight to that PDF page.

4. Map Frameworks & Folders

Drag cards in the Framework Flowchart above to model your theoretical or methodology design, and group PDFs into Theory Folders on the left.
